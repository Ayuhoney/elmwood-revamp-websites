Australian Clinical Labs operates a pathology collection centre onsite (accessed from the back entrance) which can be used by anyone in the community. No appointment is required for a pathology test.

Please take a number from the pathology stand when you arrive and you will be called when it is your turn. If a test has been recommended by your GP, we encourage you to make a follow-up appointment to discuss your results.

Onsite pathology collection

Pathology is collected at the medical centre by Australian Clinical Labs. You do not need an appointment for collection.

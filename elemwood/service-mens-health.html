We understand that men have unique needs in terms of health and the way health services are accessed. We provide specialised men’s health services.

Consultations are conducted in a confidential and private consulting room and may cover health screening, prostate checks, blood screening, blood pressure checks and sexual health checks. Our doctors also provide preventative checks and education to suit each patient.

Men’s health checks

Private consultations can cover screening, prostate health, blood pressure, blood tests and sexual health.

Health Screening

Preventative checks tailored to each patient’s needs.

Prostate Checks

Prostate assessment as part of men’s health consultations.

Blood Pressure & Bloods

Blood pressure checks and blood screening when indicated.

Sexual Health

Confidential sexual health checks in a private consulting room.

How men’s health visits work

Book a private consultation. Your GP will discuss screening and the checks that are relevant to you.

Call or book online for a men’s health appointment.

Your doctor reviews your health and completes the checks you need.

Results, education and any further tests are arranged with you.

Men’s health appointments are confidential. Elmwood Medical Centre — (02) 6056 2011.

At Elmwood Medical Centre our aim is to help you to be proactive in keeping yourself well, both now and for the future. We encourage you to take an active interest in your own health and wellbeing.

Preventative health checks can assist you and your doctor to keep watch for signs that your health may be at risk. We can also coordinate team care arrangements for patients with chronic or complex conditions, including referrals to local allied health providers.

What preventative health can help

Regular checks and health assessments help you and your GP watch for future risk and support long-term wellbeing.

Health Screening

Regular checks to screen for signs that your health may be at risk in the future.

Health Assessments

Structured health assessments tailored to your age and health needs.

Chronic Condition Care

Team care arrangements for patients with chronic or complex conditions.

Allied Health Coordination

Referrals and liaison with podiatrists, osteopaths, psychologists and other local providers.

How preventative care works

We help you stay well with planned checks, clear advice, and coordinated care when you need extra support.

Speak with reception or book online for a preventative health appointment.

Your GP reviews your health and recommends the checks that are right for you.

Results are discussed and, where needed, allied health or specialist care is arranged.

Take an active interest in your health. Book a preventative health appointment at Elmwood Medical Centre, 7 McFarland Rd, Wodonga.

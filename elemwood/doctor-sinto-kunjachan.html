About me

Dr Sinto has years of experience in General practice and in Hospital.

After completing her degree form Deakin University Melbourne, she along with her husband and two young children have settled in Wodonga. She has a keen interest in chronic Disease Management, Mental Health and General Health Promotion.

Her hobbies include reading Medical Journals and has been awarded “Jan Moore Award for her Excellence in Research" for her Project.

She works Full time and looks forward to long-term relationships with her patients.

In her leisure time, she enjoys travelling, exploring new places, cooking and having quality time with Family.

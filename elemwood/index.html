Elmwood Medical Centre

Elmwood Medical Centre is a mixed-billed clinic. Bulk-billed appointments are available with selected doctors only and will be clearly displayed next to the doctor’s name when booking through HotDoc. All over 70 Years are bulk billed for standard consultation, and those who have paid the first GAP. Additional fees apply for services such as wound dressings, ECGs, skin checks, procedures, and other non-standard consultations. Please speak with our reception team before your appointment if you would like further information about applicable fees.

About Our Medical Centre in Wodonga

Elmwood Medical Centre has been providing trusted healthcare to the Wodonga community since 2007. As a locally doctor-owned GP clinic, our team delivers high-quality, patient-centred care for individuals and families at every stage of life.

First Gap Fee — A$100 / year

To help reduce repeated out-of-pocket costs, our practice offers a First Gap Fee option. Instead of paying a gap fee at every visit, patients may choose to pay a once-per-calendar-year First Gap Fee of A$100. This is like paying the gap for two standard appointments and helps cover your standard visits for the rest of the year as per above and under 20 yrs. each year, and then bulk bill any standard consultations for the same year.

How Medicare works
  • The A$100 First Gap Fee is the patient’s out-of-pocket contribution
  • Medicare pays the rebate directly to the practice
  • As this amount represents the gap, there is no cheque or refund issued for this fee to the patient
